Description
-----------

Find a photo by describing what you remember.

"The sunset at the beach last summer"
"My dog playing in the snow"
"Birthday cake with candles"
"That hike with the waterfall"

Memorable uses bundled, on-device AI to search the visual content of your photos and videos. Build a private index on your iPhone, then search with natural language instead of scrolling through your library.

How it works
------------

Grant Photo Library access and build the local search index. Memorable indexes photos first, makes them searchable at progressive checkpoints, and then processes video frames and optional metadata enrichment.

Type a scene, object, date, season, or place. Memorable parses the query and ranks the local index. Photo and video contents and search query text stay on your iPhone.

Privacy
-------

Memorable has no developer backend, account system, analytics, tracking, ads, telemetry, or crash-report uploads.

For location search, the app sends GPS coordinates contained in indexed media metadata to Apple's CLGeocoder service to resolve place names. It does not send media contents or search query text with those requests.

The app uses Apple StoreKit to load Pro product information and to process entitlement checks, purchases, restores, refunds, and transaction updates. StoreKit exchanges the commerce data Apple requires for those services.

The local search database and folder bookmark files are excluded from iCloud and device backups.

Free features
-------------

- Search photos and videos with natural language.
- Use locally generated OCR and scene labels to improve ranking.
- Browse On This Day, Smart Albums, Surprise Me, and memory slideshows.
- Index up to 500 media items for free.

Pro features
------------

- Expand the index to 8,000 photos and videos.
- Parse dates, seasons, places, time of day, and excluded terms.
- Add up to 32 user-selected folder sources.
- Use Find Similar and near-duplicate detection with bounded, cancellable processing.
- Unlock Pro with one purchase. No subscription is required.

Requirements
------------

- iOS 17 or later
- iPhone with an A14 chip or later
- Approximately 750 MB of storage for bundled AI models

What's new in version 1.0
-------------------------

- Search photos and videos with SigLIP2.
- Index Photo Library and up to 32 selected folder sources with Pro.
- Apply date-aware and location-aware local ranking.
- Use bundled models without an additional model download.
