DESCRIPTION
-----------
Find any photo by describing what you remember.

"The sunset at the beach last summer"
"My dog playing in the snow"
"Birthday cake with candles"
"That hike with the waterfall"

Memorable uses on-device AI to understand the visual content of your photos, letting you search with natural language instead of scrolling through thousands of pictures.

HOW IT WORKS
One-time setup: Memorable scans your photo library and builds a private search index on your device. This typically takes a few minutes. After that, searches are instant.

Type a description of what you're looking for — a scene, an object, a feeling — and Memorable finds the closest matches across your entire library.

COMPLETE PRIVACY
Unlike cloud-based photo services, Memorable processes everything on your device. Your photos never leave your phone. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your memories stay yours. Location names are resolved using Apple's built-in services to improve search.

KEY FEATURES
- Natural language search: describe what you see, not tags or dates
- On-device AI: your photos never leave your device
- No subscription: pay once, use forever
- Fast: search 10,000+ photos in under a second
- Smart date filtering: "beach photos from last summer" narrows by both content and time
- Multiple sources: search your Photos library and imported folders
- Incremental indexing: new photos are automatically detected
