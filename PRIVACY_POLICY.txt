Privacy Policy for Memorable - AI Photo Search
Last updated: April 2026

SUMMARY
Your photos never leave your device. Memorable does not collect, store, or transmit any photos, search queries, or personal data.

DATA COLLECTION
Memorable collects NO data whatsoever. Specifically:
- No personal information is collected
- No photos are uploaded or transmitted anywhere
- No search queries are recorded or sent to any server
- Location coordinates are sent to Apple's CLGeocoder service to resolve place names (e.g. city, country) for location-based search
- No usage analytics or tracking of any kind
- No crash reports are sent externally
- No advertising identifiers are used
- No third-party SDKs that collect data are included

ON-DEVICE PROCESSING
All photo analysis and search functionality happens entirely on your device using Apple's Core ML framework. Your photos are processed locally to generate search embeddings (mathematical representations), which are stored only in a local database on your device.

NETWORK ACCESS
All photo analysis and search runs entirely on your device. The only network usage is Apple's CLGeocoder service, which resolves GPS coordinates and place names to improve location-based search. Only coordinates or place name text are sent -- no photos, no search queries, no personal data. Location search still works offline using previously resolved data.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS
Memorable requests access to your Photo Library solely to read photos for local indexing and display. Photos are never copied, uploaded, or shared. You can revoke this access at any time in Settings > Privacy > Photos.

FOLDER ACCESS
If you choose to add a folder source, Memorable reads images only from the folder you explicitly select. No other files or folders are accessed.

LOCAL STORAGE
Memorable stores the following data locally on your device:
- Photo embeddings (mathematical vectors for search) in a local SQLite database
- Photo metadata (dates, dimensions) for search filtering
- No original photos are copied or stored by the app

DATA DELETION
Uninstalling Memorable removes all locally stored data. You can also delete the search index from within the app at any time.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Memorable does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app update. Given that Memorable collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.

CONTACT
For questions about this privacy policy, visit the app's support page on the App Store.
