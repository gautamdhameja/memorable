**Privacy policy for Memorable: AI Photo Search**
Last updated: September 5, 2026

Memorable analyzes photos, videos, and search queries on your iPhone. The developer does not receive them or collect usage data. The app has no accounts, ads, tracking, or analytics. Technical logs stay on your device; the app does not upload them.

**Photo and folder access**

Memorable reads the photos and videos you allow it to access so you can search and view them. It does not download iCloud-only media. You can limit or revoke Photo Library access in iOS Settings.

If you add a folder, Memorable reads images in that folder and saves permission to reopen it until you remove the folder source.

**Data stored on your iPhone**

Memorable stores a search index, including image features, text found in images, dates, and locations. It also saves recent searches, smart albums, folder access permissions, and settings. AI processing runs on your device with bundled models.

The search database and folder access files use iOS file protection and are excluded from device backups.

**Apple services**

To find place names, Memorable may send GPS coordinates from your photos and videos to Apple's location services. These requests do not include media contents or search queries.

Apple processes Pro purchases and restores, and verifies purchase status. Its services handle the account, transaction, and device information needed for those functions.

**Sharing and playback**

A photo is shared only after you tap Share and choose a destination. The copy can include GPS, camera, date, and other embedded metadata. The destination's privacy policy applies to its use of the photo.

If you choose an AirPlay destination, Apple and that destination handle playback.

**Retention and deletion**

Local data stays until you remove it or delete Memorable. You can clear recent searches and remove folder sources in the app. Removing a folder source deletes its search index and saved access permission. Re-indexing rebuilds search data; it keeps saved folders.

Delete Memorable to remove its local data. This does not delete your original photos or videos.

**Changes and contact**

Policy changes are included in app updates. For privacy questions, use the support page listed on Memorable's App Store page.
